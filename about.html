WHO WE ARE

Shaping Skylines with Structural Integrity

Founded in 2008, RTP CONSTRUCTION was built on a single, uncompromising belief: that world-class architecture begins with structural perfection. Over nearly two decades, we have evolved from a specialized structural firm into a premier turnkey construction enterprise.

Our team of senior structural engineers, BIM architects, and project directors utilize advanced 3D building information modeling, eco-certified concrete matrices, and kinetic construction workflows to execute complex developments seamlessly.

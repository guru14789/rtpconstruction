WHO WE ARE

Structural Integrity, Every Time

Founded in 2008, RTP CONSTRUCTION believes great architecture starts with structural perfection. We have grown from a specialist structural firm into a full turnkey construction company.

Our engineers, BIM architects and project directors use 3D modeling and eco-certified concrete to deliver complex developments on time and with care.
